Kurt Jones Systems Ltd

Kurt Jones brand identity

The identity for a media facilities and workflow specialist. It is built on one idea, the operator's view: the person in the gallery chair who knows what a facility actually needs from the systems underneath it.

01 Strategy

The operator's view

Integrators and installers hand a facility over and leave. Kurt managed one: the studio floor, control room, gallery and post suites at the University of West London for five years. He also helped build the remote setup that kept its students' live news programme on air during the pandemic. The identity borrows its language from the gallery.

Tally light

Means this is live, look here. One red point marks what matters most, and nothing else is red.

Signal path

Shows how one thing feeds the next. Diagrams draw the programme path as the single red line.

Plain labels

What a crew reads under pressure. Short, factual, first person. Claim, then evidence.

Audiences, in order. Support and services managers at media technology vendors, then expert networks, universities and consulting firms, then direct editing and production clients.

02 Logo

A K, a j, and a tally light

The j's dot is the tally light: the one point of colour in the identity. Strokes are square cut on a 48 unit grid. The wordmark is Arial Bold converted to outlines and tracked at 0.16 em, so it never depends on an installed font.

03 Colour

Neutrals from ink to paper, one tally red

The greys carry a faint warm bias so they read as chosen, not default. Every text pairing is checked against WCAG 2.2 AA on Ink.

{{SWATCHES}}

Tally rules. At most one tally element per screen outside diagrams. Never set tally as text on paper, where it fails at 2.86:1. Ink text on a tally fill passes at 6.14:1, and that is the only filled button. Meta drops to 4.48:1 on Raised, so it never sits on Raised.

04 Type

A Swiss sans, and the mono of a timecode display

Helvetica Neue, falling back to Arial, does everything. The monospace appears only on technical labels: step numbers, eyebrows and diagram stage names. A self-hosted brand typeface is an optional upgrade, pending approval.

07 Voice

Claim, then evidence

Write
  • "For five years I managed a university's film and motion capture studios."
  • "Networking is the supporting context for my production and facilities work."
  • "I am highly competent with AI tools." Separately: "I am particular about where they belong."
  • UK English, sentence case, first person.
Never
  • Adjectives doing the work evidence should do.
  • Disclaimers about coding or networking ability.
  • Em dashes or en dashes, anywhere.
  • Location, client site names, invented figures, stock photos, logo walls.
