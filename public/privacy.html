Last updated 29 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to personal data you send through kurtjones.co.uk.

Who is responsible

Kurt Jones Systems Ltd is the controller of the personal data described here. Registered in England and Wales, company number 16509632. Registered office: C/O Aardvark Accounting, 1 Cedar Office Park, Cobham Road, Wimborne, England, BH21 7SB.

For anything about your data, email kurt@kurtjones.co.uk.

What is collected

If you use the contact form, it collects your name, email address, subject and message. If you email directly, I receive whatever your email contains.

The site has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Why, and on what basis

Your details are used only to reply to your enquiry and, where you ask, to discuss or arrange work. The lawful basis is legitimate interests: responding to people who get in touch, and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms relays contact form messages to my inbox. It is run from India on infrastructure provided by Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner, which may process data in several regions, and it checks messages for spam using CleanTalk and Akismet. Transfers of UK personal data are covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses in its data processing agreement.
  • My email provider stores the messages I receive and send.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and, like any web host, processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver and protect it.
  • YouTube supplies the showreel videos. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. Once you do, YouTube (Google) may store data on your device under its own privacy policy.

Your data is never sold or used for marketing.

How long it is kept

Enquiries are kept for 24 months from the last contact, then deleted, unless they lead to work. In that case they are kept as business records for as long as the law requires. Web3Forms keeps its own copy under its retention policy, currently a maximum of three years.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. You can also object to how it is used, or ask for its use to be restricted. Email the address above and I will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
