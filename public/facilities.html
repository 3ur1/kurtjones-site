Case study 01

How a student news programme stayed on air with the crew working apart

University of West London, 2020 to 2021

The problem

When the campus closed, a video journalism course still had a live news programme to produce and air. It was built around a physical gallery, the crew could no longer share a building, and the transmission slot did not move.

What we built

A remote production workflow that let the crew run the programme with everyone in a different place. Contribution, communication, direction and transmission were distributed, not abandoned.

The outcome

The programme continued to be produced and aired on schedule, semester after semester, with the course running as normal.

This is the problem much of the media technology industry now sells into. We solved a version of it as a team, under a hard deadline, with no budget for failure.
